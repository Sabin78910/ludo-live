## Full description
Tired of dice that feel rigged? Ludo Live is the first Ludo where you can **prove every roll is fair**.

🔒 PROVABLY FAIR DICE
Before the game starts we lock the dice with a published cryptographic hash. When the game ends, the secret is revealed
and your phone recomputes every single roll. If anything was changed, you will see it. No other Ludo app does this.

🚫 NO ADS IN MATCHES
Nothing interrupts your game. Ever.

🌐 PLAY ONLINE WITH FRIENDS
Create a room, share the 6-letter code or send the invite straight to WhatsApp. Lost connection? Rejoin and carry on;
a bot keeps your seat warm if you are away for 30 seconds.

🤖 SMART BOTS
Easy, normal and hard bots that capture, play safe and race home, for offline practice anywhere.

📱 PASS & PLAY
2 to 4 players on one phone.

⚡ QUICK MODE
One token starts on the board and the first token home wins: a full game in a few minutes.

✨ BEAUTIFUL AND SMOOTH
A 3D board, glossy tokens, rolling dice, satisfying hops and captures, confetti for the winner, sound and vibration.

🇳🇵 नेपाली र English
Play in Nepali or English.

Classic rules: roll a 6 to leave base, capture to roll again, stars are safe, three 6s lose the turn, exact roll to finish.
