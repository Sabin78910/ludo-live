## Full description (max 4000)
Tired of dice that feel rigged? Ludo Live lets you **prove every roll is fair**.

🔒 PROVABLY FAIR DICE
• Before the game starts, the dice are locked with a published cryptographic hash
• When the game ends, the secret is revealed and your phone recomputes every roll
• If a single roll was changed, the Verify fairness screen shows it

🚫 NO ADS IN MATCHES
• Nothing interrupts your game. Ever.

🌐 PLAY ONLINE WITH FRIENDS
• Create a room and share the 6-letter code, or send the invite straight to WhatsApp
• Lost connection? Rejoin and carry on
• A bot keeps your seat warm if you are away for 30 seconds

🤖 SMART BOTS
• Easy, Normal and Hard bots that capture, play safe and race home
• Practise offline, anywhere

📱 PASS & PLAY
• 2 to 4 players on one phone

⚡ QUICK MODE
• One token starts on the board and the first token home wins: a full game in a few minutes

✨ BEAUTIFUL AND SMOOTH
• A 3D board, glossy tokens and rolling dice
• Satisfying hops and captures, confetti for the winner, sound and vibration
• Sound and vibration can be turned off any time

🇳🇵 नेपाली र English
• Play in Nepali or English

Classic rules: roll a 6 to leave base, capture to roll again, stars are safe, three 6s lose the turn, exact roll to finish.

No accounts, no ads in matches, no real money. Download Ludo Live and play a game you can trust.
